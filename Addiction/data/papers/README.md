# Research corpus

The four authorized research documents for MindEvidence Lab:

1. WHO mhGAP Guideline (2023)
2. NIDA Principles of Drug Addiction Treatment
3. WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide v2.0

PDF files are not committed to the repository (see `.gitignore`).
Place them in this folder.
